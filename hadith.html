Biography of Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf An-Nawawi (رحمه الله)

631 AH – 676 AH (1233 CE – 1277 CE) • The Shaykh al-Islam, Master Muhaddith & Ascetic of Damascus

أَبُو زَكَرِيَّا يَحْيَى بْنُ شَرَفٍ النَّوَوِيُّ
🌱

Early Life in Nawa

Born in Nawa, south of Damascus. As a boy, while other children played, young Yahya wept if forced to play, preferring to recite the Holy Quran. He memorized the entire Quran before puberty.

📖

12 Lessons Every Day

In Damascus at the Rawahiyyah madrasah, he studied 12 distinct lessons daily with senior scholars across Hadith, Fiqh, Usul, syntax, and language. He slept only while seated when overcome by slumber.

⚖️

Uncompromising Zuhd

As head of Dar al-Hadith al-Ashrafiyyah, he declined any stipend. He subsisted entirely on bread and figs sent by his father. He courageously stood before Sultan Baybars to defend civilian taxes.

✨

Short Life, Eternal Barakah

Passing away at just 44 years of age without ever marrying, Allah blessed his works with unparalleled barakah. His books remain the standard texts in mosques and universities worldwide.

📚 Why The "40 Hadith" Compendium is Legendary

Many classical scholars compiled "Forty Hadith" collections based on popular virtue narrations, yet Imam an-Nawawi’s compilation stands peerless in Islamic history. Rather than focusing merely on virtues of actions (Fada'il al-A'mal), Imam an-Nawawi deliberately chose 42 ahadith where each hadith forms "a grand foundation of the religion (Qa'idah 'Adheemah min Qawa'id ad-Deen)".

Leading scholars remark that nearly all aspects of Islamic belief, worship, ethics, financial dealings, and civil conduct can be traced back to the principles enshrined within these 42 comprehensive traditions.

«يَنْبَغِي لِكُلِّ مَنْ رَغِبَ فِي الآخِرَةِ أَنْ يَعْرِفَ هَذِهِ الأَحَادِيثَ؛ لِمَا اشْتَمَلَتْ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ المُهِمَّاتِ، وَاحْتَوَتْ عَلَيْهِ مِنَ التَّنْبِيهِ عَلَى جَمِيعِ الطَّاعَاتِ» — Imam an-Nawawi in his introduction to the 40 Hadith

🏛️ Monumental Works of Imam an-Nawawi

  • 1.
    Riyad as-Salihin (رياض الصالحين): The Gardens of the Righteous, the most universally recited book of ethics and spirituality after the Quran.
  • 2.
    Al-Minhaj Sharh Sahih Muslim (شرح صحيح مسلم): The quintessential master commentary on Sahih Muslim, combining hadith derivation, grammar, and jurisprudence.
  • 3.
    Al-Adhkar (الأذكار): The premier encyclopedic compilation of prophetic supplications, remembrances, and spiritual formulas for every daily circumstance.
  • 4.
    Al-Majmu' Sharh al-Muhadhdhab (المجموع): The crowning jewel of comparative Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh Muqaran), analyzing rulings with rigorous evidential proofs.
