Mosque Kiosk TV Setup Guide (دَلِيلُ التَّثْبِيتِ عَلَى الشَّاشَاتِ)

How to mount and run this free digital display on smart monitors

📺
1. Any Smart TV Browser

Open the browser app on any Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android TV, or Amazon Fire Stick. Navigate to noorhub.truxio.bond/mosque.html and click "Launch Fullscreen TV Kiosk".

2. Raspberry Pi or Hallway PC

Launch Chrome or Chromium in auto-start kiosk mode:
chromium-browser --kiosk --incognito https://noorhub.truxio.bond/mosque.html

3. Zero Tracking & Offline Resilient

All calculations are computed locally via astronomical formulas. Even if the mosque's Wi-Fi drops, the clock, prayer timetable, and Iqamah countdowns remain 100% accurate.
