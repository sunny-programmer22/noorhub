Dhabihah (Islamic Slaughter)

Meat must be slaughtered by a Muslim, Christian, or Jewish person of faith invoking the Name of Allah (*Bismillah Allahu Akbar*), using a razor-sharp blade severing the windpipe, esophagus, and jugular veins swiftly without causing unnecessary suffering.

⚠️

The Fiqh of Stunning

Contemporary scholars and the International Islamic Fiqh Academy permit non-lethal, reversible pre-slaughter stunning only if it is proven the animal remains fully alive at the exact moment of the cut. Irreversible or penetrative captive-bolt stunning is forbidden.

🧼

Cross-Contamination Fiqh

In shared commercial kitchens, frying oil, cutting boards, grills, and utensils used for pork or non-halal meat contaminate otherwise halal food. Believers should ensure dedicated cookware or strict segregation before dining.
