The 99 Names of Allah

وَلِلَّهِ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ فَادْعُوهُ بِهَا

"And to Allah belong the best names, so invoke Him by them." (Surah Al-A'raf 7:180)

📜 Prophetic Hadith on the Virtue of Memorizing & Contemplating

عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ﷺ قَالَ: «إِنَّ لِلَّهِ تِسْعَةً وَتِسْعِينَ اسْمًا، مِائَةً إِلَّا وَاحِدًا، مَنْ أَحْصَاهَا دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ»

Narrated Abu Hurairah (RA): The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever enumerates and memorizes them (ahṣāhā) will enter Paradise."

Sahih al-Bukhari (2736), Sahih Muslim (2677) Meaning of 'Ahṣāhā': Memorizing words, contemplating their meanings, supplicating with them, and embodying their virtuous reflection.

Du'a & Supplication Etiquette

How to Supplicate (Du'a) through Asma-ul-Husna

Match your spiritual or worldly needs with the corresponding Divine Attribute as recommended by the scholars of Ahl as-Sunnah.

🤲

Seeking Forgiveness

يَا غَفَّارُ • يَا تَوَّابُ • يَا عَفُوُّ

"Yā Ghaffār, Yā Tawwāb, Yā 'Afuww: Pardon my shortcomings, wipe clean my errors, and turn toward me in mercy."

🍞

Seeking Provision & Ease

يَا رَزَّاقُ • يَا فَتَّاحُ • يَا وَهَّابُ

"Yā Razzāq, Yā Fattāḥ, Yā Wahhāb: Open for me the closed doors of halal sustenance and bless my livelihood."

🕊️

Seeking Inner Peace & Calm

يَا سَلَامُ • يَا مُؤْمِنُ • يَا لَطِيفُ

"Yā Salām, Yā Mu'min, Yā Laṭīf: Grant tranquility to my restless heart and shield my mind from anxiety."

🛡️

Seeking Protection & Justice

يَا حَفِيظُ • يَا وَكِيلُ • يَا عَدْلُ

"Yā Ḥafīẓ, Yā Wakīl, Yā 'Adl: Protect my faith and family, and suffice me against oppression and injustice."
