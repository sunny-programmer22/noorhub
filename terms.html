You are warmly invited and permitted to use NoorHub for:

  • Personal Devotion: Tracking daily Salat, recitation of Adhkar, Ramadan voluntary fasting, and personal Zakat calculations.
  • Mosque & Musalla Displays: Running the Mosque TV Kiosk Mode on public screens, prayer hall monitors, community centers, or university Islamic societies.
  • Education & Study: Utilizing the 40 Hadith of Imam Nawawi, Islamic baby name definitions, and Faraid inheritance simulations in classroom, homeschooling, or halaqah environments.

3 Prayer Times & Astronomical Calculation Disclaimer

NoorHub utilizes rigorous astronomical algorithms (incorporating solar zenith equations, latitude adjustments, and convention twilight angles from recognized bodies such as the Muslim World League, ISNA, and Umm al-Qura).

⚠️ Important Guidance on Local Prayer Schedules:

Astronomical calculations are mathematical models of solar position. Atmospheric conditions, local topography, mountain shadows, and city twilight pollution can introduce small deviations. We always recommend that worshippers prioritize their local congregation's established Adhan schedule and visual sighting protocols, especially for Fajr and Isha in high-latitude regions (above 48° North or South) during summer months.

4 Zakat & Faraid Educational Disclaimers

The Multi-Asset Zakat Calculator and Faraid Inheritance Engine are computational aids designed to help Muslims comprehend Islamic financial and estate jurisprudence. They do not constitute a formal individualized judicial verdict (Fatwa) or legal estate decree for court proceedings:

  • Zakat: Asset valuations (particularly for illiquid private shares, debts, or retirement funds) should be reviewed according to the fatwa of your local trustworthy scholar or Islamic center.
  • Faraid: Inheritance in Islam is subject to delicate family nuances, debts of the deceased, unfulfilled bequests (Wasiyyah up to 1/3), and estate probate laws in your jurisdiction. For binding estate division, consult a certified Islamic probate scholar or local Shariah council.

5 Food Additives (E-Number) Guidance

Our Halal Food and E-Number directory categorizes food additives based on recognized Islamic halal certification guidelines and biochemical manufacturing sources (plant, animal, synthetic, microbial). Because ingredient sourcing varies across regional manufacturers and industrial suppliers, users should verify packaging labels and look for trusted local halal certification logos on consumer products.
