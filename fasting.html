Authentic Du'as for Sawm

Fully vocalized Arabic with tashkeel, transliteration, English meanings, and verified canonical Hadith citations.

1. Niyyah (Intention for Fasting) At Suhoor / Heart

وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ

"Wa bi-ṣawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri Ramaḍān."

"I intend to keep the fast tomorrow for the month of Ramadan."

Reference: Traditional Sunnah Intention
2. Breaking the Fast (Sunnah Iftar) At Maghrib after 1st sip

ذَهَبَ الظَّمَأُ وَابْتَلَّتِ الْعُرُوقُ وَثَبَتَ الأَجْرُ إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ

"Dhahaba aẓ-ẓama'u wabtallatil-'urūqu wa thabatal-ajru in shā' Allāh."

"The thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills."

Sunan Abi Dawud (2357) • Sahih
3. When Breaking Fast with a Host At Guest Invitation

أَفْطَرَ عِنْدَكُمُ الصَّائِمُونَ وَأَكَلَ طَعَامَكُمُ الأَبْرَارُ وَصَلَّتْ عَلَيْكُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ

"Afṭara 'indakumus-ṣā'imūna wa akala ṭa'āmakumul-abrāru wa ṣallat 'alaykumul-malā'ikah."

"May fasting people break their fast with you, may the righteous eat your food, and may the angels send blessings upon you."

Sunan Abi Dawud (3854) • Sahih
4. Laylatul Qadr (Night of Power) Last 10 Odd Nights

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ كَرِيمٌ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

"Allāhumma innaka 'Afuwwun Karīmun tuḥibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'annī."

"O Allah, You are Most Forgiving and Generous; You love forgiveness, so forgive me."

Jami' at-Tirmidhi (3513) • Sahih

Sunnah al-Nawafil

Voluntary Fasting Guide

The year-round prophetic calendar of non-obligatory fasts that elevate faith and expiate sins.

🌕 Monthly

The White Days (Ayyām al-Bīḍ)

13th, 14th & 15th of Every Hijri Month

Named because the moon is at its fullest and brightest. Fasting three days every month is equivalent to fasting the entire year in reward, as each good deed is multiplied tenfold.

"If you fast three days of the month, fast the 13th, 14th and 15th." (Sunan an-Nasa'i 2420)
📅 Weekly

Mondays & Thursdays

Every Monday and Thursday

The Prophet ﷺ was keen on fasting Mondays and Thursdays, explaining that deeds are presented before Allah on these two days, and he loved for his deeds to be presented while fasting.

"Deeds are presented on Monday and Thursday, and I love that my deeds be presented while I am fasting." (Tirmidhi 747)
⛰️ Annual

The Day of 'Arafah

9th of Dhul-Hijjah

For those not performing Hajj pilgrimage, fasting the Day of Arafah carries the monumental virtue of expiating the minor sins of both the preceding year and the coming year.

"Fasting on the Day of 'Arafah expiates the sins of the past year and the coming year." (Sahih Muslim 1162)
🌊 Annual

The Day of 'Ashura

10th of Muharram (+ 9th Tasu'a)

Commemorates the day Allah saved Prophet Musa (Moses AS) and the Children of Israel from Pharaoh. Fasting Ashura expiates the sins of the previous year. Fasting the 9th alongside it is Sunnah.

"Fasting the day of 'Ashura, I hope that Allah will accept it as expiation for the year that came before it." (Muslim 1162)
🌟 Post-Ramadan

Six Days of Shawwal

Any 6 Days in Shawwal (after Eid)

Whoever fasts the month of Ramadan and follows it with six voluntary days of fasting during Shawwal receives the reward of fasting perpetually for an entire unbroken year.

"Whoever fasts Ramadan and then follows it with six days of Shawwal, it is as if he fasted for a lifetime." (Muslim 1164)
👑 Supreme

Fasting of Prophet Dawud (AS)

Alternate Days (Fast one, eat one)

The most beloved fasting to Allah is the fasting of Dawud (David AS). He used to fast on alternate days and never fled when meeting the adversary. It represents supreme spiritual discipline.

"The most beloved fasting to Allah is that of Dawud: he used to fast every other day." (Bukhari 1131, Muslim 1159)
Jurisprudence (Fiqh al-Siyam)

Fiqh of Fasting Essentials

Clarity on rulings, contemporary medical scenarios, nullifiers requiring Qadha or Kaffarah, and valid exemptions.

The fast (Sawm) has two non-negotiable core pillars:

  • The Intention (Niyyah): Must be made from the heart before the break of dawn (Fajr) for obligatory Ramadan fasts. Stating it aloud is not a condition; heartfelt resolve suffices.
  • Abstinence (Imsak): Refraining from food, drink, marital relations, and intentional nullifiers from the crack of dawn (Fajr) until total sunset (Maghrib).

The following nullify the fast and require making up one day (Qadha) after Ramadan without heavy expiation (Kaffarah):

  • Eating or drinking intentionally (without forgetfulness). Note: If done out of genuine forgetfulness, the Prophet ﷺ stated: "His fast is valid; Allah fed him and gave him drink." (Bukhari & Muslim).
  • Deliberately inducing vomiting by forcing oneself (unintentional vomiting does not break fast).
  • Onset of menstruation (Hayd) or post-natal bleeding (Nifas), even moments before Maghrib.
  • Nutritional intravenous (IV) fluids or glucose infusions that substitute for food/nourishment.

Engaging in sexual intercourse deliberately during the fasting daylight hours of Ramadan is a major sin that incurs Kaffarah in addition to making up the day:

The order of Kaffarah:

  1. Freeing a believing slave (not applicable today).
  2. Fasting 60 consecutive days (two lunar months) without interruption without a legitimate excuse.
  3. If physically unable due to chronic illness or old age, feeding 60 destitute persons (one average meal each or its monetary value).

Based on consensus resolutions by the International Islamic Fiqh Academy (OIC):

  • Asthma Inhalers: The majority of contemporary jurists (including Ibn Baz, Ibn 'Uthaymeen, and OIC Fiqh Academy) rule that therapeutic gas inhalers do not break the fast as the micro-droplets target bronchial airways, not digestive nutrition.
  • Eye Drops & Ear Drops: Do not invalidate the fast even if a taste reaches the throat, provided they do not pass through a ruptured eardrum into digestion.
  • Intramuscular & Subcutaneous Injections: Insulin, penicillin, analgesics, vaccines, or local dental anesthesia do not break the fast because they are non-nutritive and do not pass through the normal digestive tract.
  • Routine Blood Tests: Drawing minor vials of blood for lab diagnostics does not invalidate the fast.
  • Using the Siwak (natural tooth-stick) throughout the day without restriction.
  • Brushing teeth with toothpaste, provided one is careful not to swallow any paste or water.
  • Bathing, swimming, or pouring cold water over the head to relieve extreme summer heat.
  • Swallowing one's own natural saliva is completely unanimously permitted.
  • Rinsing the mouth and sniffing water for Wudu (without excessive exaggeration).
  • Tasting food with the tip of the tongue for seasoning/cooking, provided it is spat out immediately and nothing reaches the esophagus.

Allah says in Surah Al-Baqarah (2:185): "Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship."

  • Temporary Illness or Travel: May break fast and must make up (Qadha) day-for-day when restored or returned.
  • Pregnant or Breastfeeding Women: If fearing harm for their own health or infant's nutrition, they are exempt and make up missed days when safely able.
  • The Elderly & Chronically Terminally Ill: Who have no realistic hope of recovery are exempt from fasting and pay Fidyah: feeding one impoverished person for every missed day (approx. 1.5 kg staple food or local currency equivalent, typically $8-$12/day).
