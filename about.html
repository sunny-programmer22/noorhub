The Story of NoorHub

NoorHub is a modern, non-profit digital waqf created to restore purity, dignity, and intellectual rigor to Islamic software tools. We build accurate, beautiful, and completely tracker-free technology for Muslims, students of knowledge, and seekers worldwide.

« إِذَا مَاتَ الْإِنْسَانُ انْقَطَعَ عَنْهُ عَمَلُهُ إِلَّا مِنْ ثَلَاثَةٍ: إِلَّا مِنْ صَدَقَةٍ جَارِيَةٍ، أَوْ عِلْمٍ يُنْتَفَعُ بِهِ، أَوْ وَلَدٍ صَالِحٍ يَدْعُو لَهُ »

01 Why NoorHub Was Built

Overcoming commercial surveillance in sacred daily rituals

⚠️ The Status Quo of Mainstream Apps

In recent years, many widely downloaded Islamic apps have betrayed the trust of worshippers. Millions of Muslims discovered that their daily prayer apps were selling real-time GPS locations to data brokers, embedding commercial advertising trackers, draining device batteries, and interrupting moments of du'a with commercial banners and subscriptions.

  • ✕ Intrusive location telemetry sent to third-party databases
  • ✕ Cluttered commercial advertisements during worship
  • ✕ Proprietary closed calculations with opaque algorithms
🌿 The NoorHub Alternative

NoorHub was founded on an unapologetic principle: worship requires tranquility and privacy. No one should ever have their physical coordinates monetized when checking if it is time to face the Qibla. NoorHub runs 100% locally in your web browser. There are no tracking scripts, no ads, and zero server-side telemetry.

  • ✓ All astronomical & fiqh calculations execute on your machine
  • ✓ Permanent Waqf: completely free forever, with no monetization
  • ✓ Open, transparent, and verifiable by scholars and developers

03 Unified Platform Architecture

Nine synchronized tools built for daily Islamic life and deep study

🧭

Salat & Qibla Engine

High-precision astronomical solar declination calculations with support for all 8 recognized global conventions (MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Egyptian, Tehran, Gulf, France) and spherical trigonometric Qibla bearings.

🌙

Fasting & Sawm Companion

Real-time Suhur cutoff and Iftar countdown timers, voluntary fasting calendars (Mondays, Thursdays, Ayyam al-Bid, Ashura, Arafah), and Ramadan 30-day tracking with Taraweeh logs.

📿

Digital Tasbih & Adhkar

Haptic and audio-supported counter with authentic morning and evening supplications from Hisn al-Muslim, custom target counts, and persistent local storage that remembers your progress.

📚

40 Hadith of Imam Nawawi

Complete Arabic text with vocalized vowels, full English translations, hadith numbers, authentic grade citations, and spiritual commentary on foundational Islamic ethics and creed.

💰

Multi-Asset Zakat Engine

Calculates 2.5% zakat obligations across liquid cash, gold, silver, traded stock equities, mutual funds, real estate inventory, and business merchandise, evaluating against both gold (85g) and silver (595g) nisabs.

📜

Faraid Inheritance Calculator

Algorithmic implementation of Quranic inheritance shares (Surah an-Nisa, Ayahs 11, 12, and 176) with handling of primary heirs, residue ('asabah), and classic radd / 'awl adjustments.

✨

Islamic Baby Names (Ism Finder)

Over 1,000 carefully curated Quranic and Sahabah names with authentic Arabic spelling, root meanings, phonetic pronunciation guides, and gender filtering for expecting Muslim parents.

🥗

Halal Food & E-Number Guide

Searchable database of hundreds of food additive E-numbers with clear classifications (Halal, Haram, Mushbooh) detailing animal vs plant origins, gelatin extraction sources, and emulsifiers.

🖥️

Mosque TV Kiosk Mode

Full-screen, high-contrast digital display optimized for masjid TV screens and community monitors, presenting dynamic Adhan and Iqamah times, next prayer countdowns, and hadith of the day.
