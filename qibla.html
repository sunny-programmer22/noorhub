🧭 Sensor Calibration & Accuracy Guidelines

♾️ Figure-8 Calibration

If compass readings jump or drift, move your phone through the air in a horizontal figure-8 pattern 3 times to recalibrate the internal magnetometer.

🧲 Avoid Magnetic Interference

Magnetic phone wallet cases, laptops, speakers, or steel rebar in concrete floors distort digital compasses. Step 2 meters away from metallic objects.

📱 Hold Phone Completely Flat

For maximum astronomical azimuth accuracy, place your phone flat on the palm of your hand or on a flat wooden prayer mat facing parallel to the ground.

Classical Islamic Jurisprudence

Fiqh of Facing the Qibla (استقبال القبلة)

Scholarly consensus across the Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali schools of thought

فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ ۚ وَحَيْثُ مَا كُنتُمْ فَوَلُّوا وُجُوهَكُمْ شَطْرَهُ

"So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Haram. And wherever you [believers] are, turn your faces toward it [in prayer]." — Surah Al-Baqarah (2:144)

⚖️ 1. Condition of Validity (Shart as-Salat)

Facing the Qibla is an absolute condition for the validity of obligatory (Fard) and confirmed voluntary prayers when able. Jurists distinguish two categories:

  • 'Ayn al-Ka'bah (Exact Target): Obligatory for those inside the Sacred Mosque (Masjid al-Haram) who can see the Kaaba.
  • Jihat al-Ka'bah (General Direction): Sufficient for those far away from Makkah. A slight deviation (such as within 30-45 degrees) does not invalidate prayer according to majority consensus, as the Prophet ﷺ stated: "Whatever is between the East and West is Qibla" (Tirmidhi 342, for Madinah).

✈️ 2. Rules for Travel, Airplanes & Moving Vehicles

For traveling Muslims:

  • Voluntary (Nafl) Prayers on a vehicle: One may pray in whichever direction the vehicle is moving, following the Sunnah of the Prophet ﷺ on his riding animal (Sahih al-Bukhari 1098).
  • Obligatory (Fard) on Airplanes / Ships: One must endeavor to start facing the Qibla using in-flight compasses or this tool. If the plane turns during prayer, one should adjust if possible, but one's prayer remains valid.

❓ 3. When in Doubt: The Duty of Ijtihad

If a Muslim finds themselves in an unfamiliar location without tools, they must exercise their best honest judgment (Ijtihad) using the sun, stars, or landmarks. If they discover later that they were mistaken, their prayer is fully valid and does not need to be repeated (Al-Mughni, Ibn Qudamah).

☀️ 4. The Sun Over Kaaba (Astronomical Alignment)

Twice each solar year, the Sun reaches the exact zenith (overhead point) directly above the Kaaba (declination 21.42° N):

  • May 27 / 28: At 12:18 PM Makkah Time (09:18 UTC)
  • July 15 / 16: At 12:27 PM Makkah Time (09:27 UTC)

At these exact moments, in every continent where the sun is visible, any vertical object's shadow casts directly opposite the Kaaba, providing an infallible historical astronomical verification.
