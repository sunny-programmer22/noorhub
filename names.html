Prophetic Naming Etiquette & The Sunnah on the 7th Day

The noble blueprint established by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ for welcoming a newborn into the Ummah with dignity, blessing, and gratitude.

١

1. Bestowing a Noble Name

The Sunnah encourages giving the child their permanent name on the 7th day after birth (or upon birth, as the Prophet ﷺ named his son Ibrahim on the day he was born). The name forms a lifelong spiritual mantle.

«كُلُّ غُلَامٍ رَهِينَةٌ بِعَقِيقَتِهِ تُذْبَحُ عَنْهُ يَوْمَ سَابِعِهِ وَيُحْلَقُ وَيُسَمَّى» (أبو داود)
٢

2. Tahneek (التَّحْنِيك)

Taking a sweet date, softening a minute fraction with saliva or water, and gently placing a tiny sweet trace on the baby’s upper palate. The Sahabah would bring their newborns to the Messenger ﷺ who performed Tahneek and supplicated for Barakah.

٣

3. Al-Aqeeqah (العَقِيقَة)

A verified Sunnah Mu'akkadah sacrifice of gratitude on the 7th day: two sheep or goats for a baby boy, and one sheep or goat for a baby girl. The meat is distributed among family, friends, and the impoverished.

٤

4. Shaving Hair & Sadaqah

Shaving the newborn baby’s head smoothly on the 7th day, weighing the fine hair, and donating its equivalent weight in silver (or cash equivalent) as voluntary charity (Sadaqah) to the poor, symbolizing purity and renewal.

✅ The Most Beloved & Recommended Categories

  • 1. Names of Servitude to Allah (Takhbeed): The Prophet ﷺ stated: "The most beloved of names to Allah are 'Abdullah' (Servant of Allah) and 'Abdur-Rahman' (Servant of the Most Merciful)." (Sahih Muslim). Any name beginning with 'Abd' followed by one of Allah’s 99 Names.
  • 2. Names of the Noble Prophets: Naming after the Messengers of Allah (e.g. Muhammad, Ahmad, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa, Yusuf, Dawud, Yahya). The Prophet ﷺ named his own son Ibrahim.
  • 3. Names of the Righteous Companions: Emulating the giants of piety and sacrifice: Abu Bakr, Umar, Uthman, Ali, Bilal, Khadijah, Aisha, Fatimah, Maryam, Asma, Zaynab.

⚠️ Prohibited & Discouraged Names in Islam

  • 1. Exclusively Divine Names: Names that belong exclusively to Allah without 'Abd' prefix (e.g. Al-Khaliq [The Creator], Ar-Rahman, Al-Quddus, Malik al-Amlak [King of Kings]).
  • 2. Servitude to Creation (Shirk): Any name meaning slave/servant to other than Allah (e.g. Abd al-Nabi, Abd al-Ka'bah, Abd al-Rasul).
  • 3. Depressing, Ugly, or Ominous Names: The Prophet ﷺ consistently changed names that signified gloom or arrogance. He changed a companion's name from 'Harb' (War) to 'Silm' (Peace), and 'Hazn' (Roughness) to 'Sahl' (Gentle/Easy).
